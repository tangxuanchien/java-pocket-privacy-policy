Privacy Policy

Java Pocket · vn.txclabs.nokia
Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Java Pocket ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App lets you pick a classic-style handset and run J2ME (Java ME) games on it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we process

  • Games you import. When you tap "Import game", the App uses Android's file picker and only receives the .jar / .jad file you choose. The game is installed and stored inside the App's private storage on your device, together with its settings (screen size, keys) and save data. We do not upload your games or save data anywhere. Removing a game in the App or uninstalling the App deletes them.
  • Games you run. Imported games run on your device inside the App's built-in Java ME runtime. A game may itself connect to the internet if it was made to do so (for example to download levels or post a score). Such connections go to the game's own servers, not to us, and are governed by that game's publisher.
  • Game catalog. The Explore tab lists games from a catalog bundled with the App. To show cover pictures, the App loads images from the websites that host them (such as downloadwap.com, agneay.github.io and prodigits.co.uk). Like any web request, this makes your IP address and basic request details visible to those sites. When you tap a game to download it, its page opens in your browser or another app; what happens there is governed by that website.
  • Advertising. The App shows ads (banner, native, interstitial and app-open ads) served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • App configuration. The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings (such as whether ads are on and which ad units to use) without an app update. This uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device and app information.
  • Stored on your device. Your settings (language, theme, vibration, the phone you picked, favourite games), your ad consent choice and a launch counter are kept locally on your device and are not sent to us.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or browsing history.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and ad consent): how Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase Remote Config (app configuration): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Websites hosting game covers and downloads listed in the catalog: their own privacy policies apply when the App loads a cover picture or when you open a game's page.

4. Permissions

  • Internet — to load ads, catalog cover pictures and the App's configuration, and for games that use the network.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) — used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Vibration — for key-press feedback and for games that vibrate the phone.

The App does not ask for storage permission: imported files come only through the Android file picker.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Data retention

Imported games, their save data and your settings stay on your device until you remove them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Advertising and configuration data is retained by Google as described in its policies.

7. Your rights

You can clear the App's data or uninstall it at any time to stop all data processing. You can manage ad personalisation in Settings → Ads privacy settings and in your device's Android settings. Contact us with any privacy request.
